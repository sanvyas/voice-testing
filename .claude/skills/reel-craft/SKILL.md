# Reel craft: learn the method, never copy the visuals

Source: seven public reels read with the `instagram-post-reader` skill (caption, counts, sample comments, frames viewed; audio was not transcribed). Counts are as of the fetch, Nov 2026. Comment counts include low-quality and off-topic comments, so treat ratios as a rough signal only.

## What the seven did

| # | Format | Likes / comments | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cinematic brand film (absurd metaphor: people burdened in a gym; relief when the product arrives) | 3,625 / 175 | One visual metaphor for the pain, one-line caption, "tag someone who needs this" CTA |
| 2 | Motion-graphics explainer (objects fly in, missed-call moments: driving, holiday), kinetic text | 265 / 17 | Escalating list of life's demands, then "a call still jumps the queue"; long story caption; product UI last |
| 3 | Founder to camera + big number ("100 million calls") + app walkthrough | 461 / 16 | Milestone announcement for a new feature; number reveal as proof; UI to finish |
| 4 | Creator skit (actor says everyday call lines, subtitled) | 1,010 / 24 | Personal testimony, relatable lines, tagging, trending music |
| 5 | Feature launch ("Actions": handling a call is half the job) | 355 / 168 | Reframe the job, floating action chips, real screens with Call back/Add to calendar; ends with a question |
| 6 | Influencer explainer: talking head + b-roll + live chat demo | 384 / 92 | Hook question, live demo of the AI talking in the viewer's language, "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send the link" |
| 7 | Creator + stock/AI b-roll, a sourced spam stat | 393 / 29 | "Gatekeeper" idea, stat infographic with source, hook question "Hate guessing calls?" |

## Building blocks (use these)
1. **Hook in 1.5 seconds.** A question, a pain line, a sound (a ringing phone), or a metaphor. Never a logo first. (Equal's reels open with "Sorry to interrupt your scroll", a gym metaphor, or a face saying a relatable line.)
2. **One idea per reel.** One pain, one feature, one number. Equal's best structure: pain, reframe, product screen, close.
3. **Kinetic text, one key word highlighted.** Words appear in time with the idea; the keyword takes the brand colour. Captions stay inside the safe area.
4. **Rhythm: a visual change every 1.5 to 3 seconds**, and alternate light and dark scenes so the eye resets.
5. **Show the product doing the job.** A real-looking screen with action buttons (Call back, WhatsApp, To-do) beats a feature list. Show the result for the owner (one line, one tap).
6. **One number, shown big.** A counter that climbs, then the arithmetic. If it is invented, label it **उदाहरण** and say numbers will differ.
7. **Show it in the viewer's language.** Hinglish or Hindi lines, as a live transcript or chat. (Reel 6's live demo in Hindi chat did the most to make it feel real.)
8. **A specific comment prompt.** Equal's keyword CTA and its direct question drew the most comments relative to likes. Ask for something small: a keyword, a number, a choice.
9. **Sound.** A ring, a ding, soft bed. Short and quiet under any voice.
10. **Length 15 to 30 seconds** for a single idea; the end card takes 5 to 6 seconds.

## Checklist before shipping
- Hook works muted, first frame readable on a phone.
- One idea only. Keyword highlighted. Cut every 1.5 to 3 s.
- Product screen shown; owner result shown.
- Every number labelled as an example or sourced.
- Munshi says it is an AI; no promise of "never miss a call", no price or discount promised.
- Comment prompt is specific; DM follow-up ready (see the captions files).

## Do not copy
- Their scenes, actors, logos, colours, lines or music. Use our own: green/ink, the म bubble, Hindi copy, our own examples.
- **AI-generated lookalikes of real celebrities or founders** (reel 7 used them). Avoid: it risks a false endorsement and platform action.
- **Regional or state references used as a joke.** Two of the seven reels drew hundreds of abusive comments over a regional reference in a related video. Keep examples neutral.
- Claims we cannot back: testimonials, "100 million calls", compatibility with other apps (a viewer asked Equal whether it works alongside Truecaller; answer it only after testing ours).

## Open questions this study could not answer
Audio and voice-over (not transcribed), watch time and shares (not public), and what the captions' hashtags did for reach.

## Munshi format library (vary the format, not just the words)
Do not make a batch with one look. Each reel should use a different format and prove a different offer. Built so far (Remotion, `reels5.tsx`, `reels6.tsx`):
- **24-hour dial** (24x7: sleep, travel, lunch, family, busy line)
- **Self-sorting inbox** (spam set aside, "Needs you" highlighted, WhatsApp-answered, always-ring VIP)
- **Race for the order** (two shops, progress bars: the missed call loses the order)
- **Call-recording player** with live captions (polite, price from board, no promise, Hindi/English, summary + WhatsApp confirmation). Needs voices with a commercial licence: the free Hindi Piper voices (AI4Bharat/IIT Madras data) are non-commercial only, so do not use them in ads; use the owner's own recording, Google Cloud TTS or Chatterbox (DeepInfra).
- **Photo of the rate list -> board; spoken update; 3-step setup**
- Earlier: end-to-end story (F1), KPIs (F3), bottleneck list (F4), no-misbehaving (F5), number reveal (G3), phone-ring choice (G1), half-the-job action chips (G2), inside-a-call (G4).
Ideas not yet built: owner's day vs Munshi's day split screen, festival rush (many calls at once), "what Munshi never says" strike-through list, a customer-language montage, weekly report card, WhatsApp-first query handling.
