# Munshi on Instagram: batch J (5 value-led reels)

Every reel follows one shape: **a moment the owner recognises (with the rupees it costs) → what normally happens → what Munshi and WhatsApp do about it, on the real screens → what gets better → "and more you can do" → comment keyword.** All shops, names, times and amounts are examples (उदाहरण). Munshi says it is an AI in every one.

| Reel | The moment | The cost shown | The fix shown | Keyword |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J1 · एक ऑर्डर की कहानी (38 s) | 7:42 pm rush, phone rings, hands full | ₹2,350 order goes to the next shop | Munshi takes the order, caller gets a WhatsApp message, owner taps Call back at 8:05 | ORDER |
| J2 · वही 5 सवाल (38 s) | 14 calls, 9 were the same 5 questions | 9 interruptions mid-billing | Owner tells it once; Munshi answers; caller gets address and hours in writing; owner sees only the 5 real calls | SAWAAL |
| J3 · diary बनाम Munshi (35 s) | "10 kg था या 5 kg?" | An angry customer, a lost slip | Structured order card (name, number, items, delivery); one-tap WhatsApp reply; full conversation kept | DIARY |
| J4 · रविवार का खाना (35 s) | Family lunch, shop phone buzzes | A half-eaten lunch, or a lost call | Three calls handled; at 4 pm one line, one Call back | FAMILY |
| J5 · वो ग्राहक जो चला गया (36 s) | Tuesday wedding-sweets enquiry, forgotten by Friday | ₹18,000 booked elsewhere | The enquiry stays on top as "Needs you" with "3 दिन से बाकी"; one-tap WhatsApp; mark done | FOLLOWUP |

**What is real in the screens:** the call card (Needs you / Good to know / Not worth it, Call back and WhatsApp buttons), the order card (items, quantities, delivery, address), "N दिन से बाकी" on a waiting call, ready WhatsApp replies that only acknowledge or ask for a missing detail, the thread per customer, the "हो गया" button, the full conversation on each call, and shop info the owner entered once. The WhatsApp message the caller gets is the product's own text (below).
**Not yet proven:** WhatsApp is built but **has not been tested on a live Meta account**, and no phone line is connected yet. Hold the "14 दिन मुफ़्त" call to action until both work (see the checklist in the portal).

The message every caller gets on WhatsApp after a call (the product's text, which we changed today from "We missed your call", because that was wrong when Munshi had answered):
> Hi, this is the AI assistant of *{shop}*. Your call is noted. Address: … Hours: … Reply here if you want to add anything and the owner will get back to you.
It never carries a price. The owner's reply buttons never confirm an order, a price or a time: they only acknowledge or ask for a missing detail.

## J1 · एक ऑर्डर की कहानी  `reels/reel-J1-one-order.mp4`

हिसाब (उदाहरण): महीने में सिर्फ़ 2 ऑर्डर बचें, हर ₹2,350 के, तो बिक्री ₹4,700 और मुनाफ़ा (15% मानें) लगभग ₹705। आपके आँकड़े अलग होंगे।
