# Munshi content playbook: show the value, then the fix

## Why the first batch fell short, and the rule now
The first batch explained *how Munshi works*. A viewer has to see *their own loss* first. The rule for every reel from now on:

1. **Recognise.** A specific moment the owner has lived (a time, a place, a thing in their hands) and the rupees at stake. They should think "that is me" in 2 seconds, with the sound off.
2. **What usually happens.** Show the consequence plainly: the order goes next door, the slip is lost, the lunch is cold.
3. **The fix, on the real screens.** The call, the WhatsApp message the caller gets, the one-line card, the one-tap reply.
4. **What gets better.** A before/after the viewer can say back in one sentence, or a small honest sum labelled उदाहरण.
5. **"And more you can do."** Two or three other things from the list below, never the whole feature list.
6. **One small comment prompt.**
Test before posting: *If I am a kirana, salon or factory owner, do I know in 3 seconds this is about me? Do I know what I would do differently on Monday?*

## Idea bank: 24 more reels, by the owner's moment
Each line is *hook (what they recognise) → the cost → the fix*. All amounts are examples.
**By trade**
1. **Salon: "Bridal enquiry, hands in someone's hair."** Hook: "सिर में रंग, हाथ में ब्रश, और ₹15,000 के bridal का call।" Cost: the booking goes to the next salon. Fix: card + WhatsApp "आपकी enquiry नोट है, तारीख बताइए" + call back after the client.
2. **Clinic: "Patient in the chair, 6 new patients' calls waiting."** Fix: Munshi notes name and problem, never gives medical advice, says the doctor will call. Show that line: "डॉक्टर बताएँगे।"
3. **Garage: "Under the car, customer asks the estimate."** Hook: "गाड़ी के नीचे हैं, फ़ोन ऊपर बज रहा है।" Fix: model of car and fault noted; owner replies after.
4. **Coaching: "Admission enquiry during class."** Hook: "क्लास चल रही है, पापा का call: batch कब से?" Fix: batch timings from shop info; demo class enquiry as Needs you.
5. **Factory/contractor: "₹60,000 quotation enquiry on the floor."** Fix: item, quantity, deadline noted; owner calls with the quote.
6. **Sweet shop: "Diwali week, 40 calls."** Fix: sorting; festival order card; WhatsApp with the address. (Do not claim it handles unlimited calls at once.)
7. **Tailor: "मेरा ब्लाउज़ तैयार है क्या?" ×10.** The same question ten times a day; the owner updates once, Munshi answers what the owner has told it.
8. **Wholesaler: "Rate by quantity."** Honest angle: Munshi takes the party's name, item and quantity and the owner quotes; never a rate it was not told.
**By moment**
9. **Night 11 pm enquiry** (show the next-morning list). 10. **Rain, shop crowded.** 11. **Owner on a bike.** 12. **Owner sick or at a wedding.** 13. **New staff, did not know the rate.** 14. **Shop closed Sunday, calls keep coming.** 15. **Lunch break.** 16. **Festival eve.** 17. **Going on holiday for a week:** "दुकान सँभाल सकते हैं, फ़ोन नहीं?" 18. **First day of the month, supplier payment calls.**
**By doubt (the panel's top reasons to say no)**
19. **"Will my customers hang up on a machine?"** Honest answer reel: it says it is an AI in the first line, speaks politely, takes the name and need; the owner reads and calls back. (Becomes much stronger once we show a real hang-up count.)
20. **"My staff already answers."** Hook: "आपका भतीजा 3 बजे स्कूल से लौटता है।" Fix: Munshi covers only the gaps (20 seconds, nobody picked).
21. **"I only answer my regulars."** Import the phone book as always-ring: Munshi answers strangers only.
22. **"What does forwarding cost?"** Plain explainer (after checking with the carriers): the one code, how to switch it off.
23. **"I am not technical."** A real 10-minute set-up on a phone, unedited.
24. **"Is my customers' data safe?"** What is kept (text, not audio), delete-my-data button, privacy page.

## Formats beyond a reel (so the feed does not look the same)
- **Carousel "diary page vs Munshi card"** (saveable), **"आज की 14 calls" infographic**, **"5 sentences Munshi never says"** (rules).
- **Stories:** poll "आज कितनी call छूटीं? 0-2 / 3-5 / 5+"; quiz "यह call किस खाने में जाएगी: Needs you / Good to know / Not worth it?"; countdown to the launch.
- **Missed-call loss calculator** (a simple web page): the owner enters calls missed a day, the share that are orders, an average order and a margin, and sees a monthly figure and what it means, with every assumption editable. Share the link in the bio. This turns "recognise the loss" into the owner's own number. I can build it as a hosted page next.
- **"Call our demo Munshi"** with a number in the bio (needs the phone line), the strongest answer to "let me try it on my phone".
- **Talking-head in Hindi** from a real person explaining one idea in 30 seconds; **screen recordings** of the real app (not mock-ups) once the phone line is on.
- **Owner stories**: only from real pilot shops, with written consent. Until then, label the stories "उदाहरण".
- **Comment-to-DM:** the keyword on each reel; reply with the DM text in captions-J.md within a few hours.
- **Seasons:** Diwali, wedding season, Eid, Navratri, school admissions, exam time: pick the trade the season hits.

## To make the content true and strong: what to build next (in order)
1. **A "calls Munshi caught" count and a "callers who hung up straight after the greeting" count in the app.** It gives real before/after numbers for the reels, and answers the biggest worry (hang-ups) with the owner's own data.
2. **A weekly summary** (the day-7 report promised in `pricing.md`) pushed to WhatsApp.
3. **Reminders** for waiting calls ("गुप्ता जी: 2 दिन से बाकी"), so a follow-up cannot be forgotten.
4. **A public demo number** for "call it now".
5. **Connect WhatsApp and the phone line, then film real screens.** Until then, every screen in these reels is a faithful recreation of the app, labelled as an example.
